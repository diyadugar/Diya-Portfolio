01

Let people browse before adding a pet

The question

Should users add a pet before seeing any services?

What we chose

Explore first; ask for the pet only when a service needs it.

Why

Less friction for new users, and we still get the info to personalise booking.

02

Keep the returning-user path short

The question

Should returning users pick their pet again on the landing page?

What we chose

Remove that step; pet selection comes later in the flow.

Why

Returning users should reach their service without extra taps.

03

Reports lives apart from Booking

The question

Should vaccine info sit inside booking history?

What we chose

Reports for health records; Booking for appointments.

Why

They answer different questions — apart, they're easier to understand.

04

One booking sequence for all

The question

Should every service get its own flow?

What we chose

Shared steps: details, pet, provider, slot, summary, payment, confirmation.

Why

Less to learn; service-specific steps can still adapt.

05

Paid isn't the same as confirmed

The question

Is a successful payment enough to call it confirmed?

What we chose

Show payment success and booking confirmation as separate states.

Why

Paying and a confirmed vet slot aren't always the same moment.

06

Use the existing design system

The question

Should Pet Care get its own visual style?

What we chose

Build in Figma with Onsurity's design system.

Why

It should feel like part of the app, not bolted on.

07

Design for now and later

The question

How should it fit while pet insurance isn't live?

What we chose

A proposed wellness offer that respects today's Select context.

Why

A clear journey without promising insurance that doesn't exist yet.
