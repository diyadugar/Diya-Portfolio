A Little About Me

I'm Diya, a Visual Communication designer with a strong interest in how design shapes the way people think, feel and interact with the world around them.

For me, design goes far beyond creating something visually appealing. It's about understanding people, identifying problems and finding meaningful ways to solve them.

My approach brings together an eye for visual detail, an understanding of user perspectives and an interest in the strategy behind a product. Whether it's building an interface, shaping a brand or exploring a new idea, I enjoy looking at the bigger picture just as much as the finer details.

I aspire to become a product designer who can connect these different perspectives to create thoughtful, intuitive and impactful digital experiences.

Because great design isn't just about how something looks. It's about how it works, who it serves and the value it creates.
