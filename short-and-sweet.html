Short and Sweet

Short and Sweet — Publication Design.

Overview: SHORT AND SWEET is a visually rich and thoughtfully designed recipe book that explores the art of dessert-making through a modern lens. This publication celebrates the fusion of culinary creativity and elegant presentation, focusing on clean layout, typography, and high-end food styling. The project highlights the importance of making recipes not only delicious but also visually appealing, turning each page into an experience. The book reflects a global perspective on contemporary dessert culture while remaining accessible to readers of all skill levels. Extensive research, collaborative efforts, and a passion for design and food were central to its creation.

Tools: Adobe InDesign, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop. Duration: 2 weeks.

Why this title? The title "Short and Sweet" perfectly captures the essence of this publication design project through a clever play on words. On one hand, it refers to the nature of the content: concise, easy-to-follow dessert recipes that are quick to make yet rich in flavor and visual appeal. On the other hand, it literally connects to the subject of the book: sweet treats, many of which are small in size or simple in preparation. The phrase also sets a light, inviting tone that aligns with the approachable and elegant design of the book. By choosing the title that is both playful and meaningful, the project reflects a balance between minimalism and indulgence, making the content engaging for a wide audience without overwhelming them.

No. of pages: 52. Dimensions: 8.2in x 11.6in. Paper: 80gsm matte. Type: Perfect binding.

Process followed. Stage 1: topic selection, research, data collection, choice of title, devising visual language. Stage 2: digital layouts, dummy creation, modification, actual size dummy.
